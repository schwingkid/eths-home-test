THE PLANNED bYTCoin PILOT

ETHS students will help design the first version.

Once funded, the planned winter pilot will test how teaching, mentoring, learning and participation can be recognized. The ledger, rules and experiences will be shaped with students before the model is offered to future YTC clubs.

bYTCoin will not be cryptocurrency or cash. Attendance, teaching hours and leadership progress will remain separately verified. Recognition should celebrate growth—not manufacture it.

FOR TODAY

The doorway is open. The room behind it will be built carefully.

Current meeting information and member resources will continue to come directly from club leadership while the portal and funded pilot are developed.
