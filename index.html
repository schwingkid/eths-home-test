Learn. Teach. Lead. Repeat.

Where students become teachers, become leaders, and then keep becoming.

At ETHS, students learn engineering, teach what they know, serve their community, and discover that a skill learned after school can matter far beyond the clubroom.

WHAT HAPPENS HERE

Technology gives students something real to master. Teaching gives them responsibility for someone else.

This restores the fuller practical picture from the original ETHS build while keeping the pared-down site focused on the student leadership model.

THE LEADERSHIP PATHWAY

Freshmen arrive. Leaders graduate.

The same path, every year, for every student willing to walk it. Students call it the club having generations.

1

Learn

Pick a bench. Build something real.

2

Teach

Show the next student how. Teaching is the badge of mastery here.

3

Lead

Run a bench, a project or an event, then take responsibility for other students.

4

Repeat

Train your replacement. Hand the club to the next generation.

WHERE IT LEADS

Different paths. The same starting point.

Former YTC students carry the clubroom with them — the habit of learning something, taking responsibility for it, and helping the next person.
