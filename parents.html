PARENTS · OVERVIEW

A club where learning turns into responsibility.

Students do not have to arrive as experts. YTC gives them a place to learn something real, become useful to someone else, and gradually take ownership of work that matters.

WHY YTC

The goal is bigger than the technology.

Computers, robotics, fabrication and engineering give students something concrete to master. The deeper model is the handoff: learn from someone older, teach someone younger, then grow into mentoring and leadership.

THE EXPERIENCE

What students actually do.

The experience is not a separate page. It is part of understanding the club: what comes into the room, what students do with it, and where that can lead.

Learn by doing.

Students work with computers, robotics, fabrication, coding, troubleshooting and other practical technology.

Teach it forward.

Students turn what they know into lessons, mentoring and community teaching. That is where technical confidence starts becoming leadership.

Meet a bigger world.

University and international relationships can bring instructors, peers and new perspectives into the ETHS clubroom while the student experience stays rooted in Evanston.

ABOUT THE CLUB

Local roots. A much wider horizon.

YTC at ETHS is built around a simple cycle: students learn, teach, lead and train the next group. The wider YTC network matters because it can create more people to learn from, more people to teach, and more serious opportunities for students to grow.

But the center of this site is the Evanston club — this room, these students, this community, and the pipeline we are building here.

What parents should know

The club is designed to let students enter at different skill levels. Advancement comes from growth, reliability and what a student can actually be trusted to do — not from arriving with a résumé already built.
