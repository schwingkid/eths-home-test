STUDENT STORY · NOEMI

One teaching role can change what a student imagines doing next.

Noemi's storyTeaching robotics changed what came next.
A real YTC pathway

From teaching robotics to pursuing robotics in college.

Noemi Soto served as an International Robotics Instructor with YTC, leading introductory Spanish-language robotics sessions for high-school students in Mexico.

Her classes used programming, Arduino builds and troubleshooting — not just to finish projects, but to help students become more independent problem-solvers.

She later carried that experience into collegiate robotics, including work with a NASA-affiliated competition team.

NAMIBIA · JULY 2025

Remote teaching became an in-person educational exchange.

From July 20–31, 2025, eight ETHS students traveled to Namibia with YTC. Five were active robotics instructors who had experience teaching students internationally; three documented the exchange as photographers.

What the students actually did

At Open Doors Education Center in Swakopmund, ETHS students worked directly with local youth using a rotating model: robotics on one day, computer refurbishing on the next.

In Windhoek, the group delivered Arduino kits to a newly launched program at Zanele Mbeke Private Primary School and met students there in person for the first time.

The trip also included a visit to the U.S. Embassy in Windhoek to discuss the work and possible future collaboration.

LEADERSHIP

The student pathway is simple to understand and hard to fake.

Students move forward by becoming more capable and more useful to other people. Leadership is not a title handed out for showing up; it grows from what a student can actually be trusted to do.

1
LearnBuild a real technical or teaching skill.
2
TeachExplain it well enough for someone else to succeed.
3
MentorHelp another student keep progressing.
4
LeadTake responsibility for people, work and continuity.

Recognition supports the journey. It does not replace it.

The Leadership Ladder is about growth and responsibility. bYTCoin is a separate recognition layer that can acknowledge teaching, mentoring, participation and milestones.

bYTCoin is not cryptocurrency and it is not cash. It is an internal YTC recognition system used for program experiences and rewards. Students do not buy their way up the Leadership Ladder. Read the bYTCoin explainer →
