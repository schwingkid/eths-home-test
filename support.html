THE CHICAGO FOUNDATION OF THE GLOBAL LEARNING ALLIANCE

At ETHS, the Alliance’s architecture was visible before it had a name.

Through YTC’s long relationship with Evanston Township High School, YTC was already building the kind of Global Learning Center the Alliance would later connect: a place where students acquire hands-on STEM skills, teach younger students, support peers and share knowledge across borders.

An ETHS volunteer fair helped connect YTC with Evanston Lighthouse Rotary, Northwestern University and Haile-Manas Academy in Ethiopia. YTC supplied robotics materials, and ETHS student-teachers helped train Ethiopian students. Those students did not remain recipients; they later joined YTC-directed teaching in Namibia and Nigeria.

That relationship was a pre-Alliance precursor. A global alliance built around YTC’s learner-to-teacher model had long been founder David Finkel’s dream. When Prosper Tornyi brought his experience in Africa to YTC, David recognized an opportunity to begin putting that vision into motion. Prosper became an early catalyst who helped incubate the developing Alliance. Eslien Tsuses then made the concept operational by building its first working international partnership in Namibia. ETHS made the Chicago side institutional by investing in its students as international teachers and collaborators.

Today, YTC@ETHS is being developed as YTC’s first Flagship Global Learning Center—the working model future clubs can learn from and adapt. It remains the local ETHS chapter, not a national headquarters.

THE YTC@ETHS COMMUNITY TEAM

You do not have to teach robotics to strengthen the club.

YTC@ETHS is student-led, but students should not have to carry every adult responsibility around the program. We are building a small community advisory team of parents, alumni and Evanston professionals who can take ownership of a defined need and help the club grow.

Marketing + storytelling

Help turn real student work into clear stories, photographs, video, news and community awareness.

Design + digital

Help improve the website, visual materials, presentations and the tools students and supporters use.

Fundraising + grants

Help identify opportunities, prepare proposals, build donor relationships and strengthen local support.

Partnerships + opportunities

Connect ETHS students with universities, companies, nonprofits, speakers, mentors and meaningful experiences.

Equipment + operations

Help source, organize and move useful technology, supplies and the practical resources that keep projects working.

Mentors + speakers

Share a skill, career path, idea or experience that can widen what an ETHS student imagines doing next.

This is an advisory working team, not a second governing board and not an open-ended volunteer commitment. The invitation is simple: bring a useful skill, choose a defined need and help carry it.

bYTCoin · Planned Pilot

FUND THE MODEL BEFORE LAUNCH

Let ETHS students help build a system future clubs can use.

A planned winter pilot, at ETHS only, and only once it is funded: students help shape how learning, teaching, mentoring and participation are recognized, then test a model other YTC clubs can adapt. Local businesses and families will be able to contribute experiences and rewards, not only cash. Club leadership and program facilitators manage it together, so it never becomes another administrative burden.

Important: bYTCoin will not be cryptocurrency, cash or a way to purchase advancement. Teaching hours and leadership progress must still be verified. The pilot is designed to recognize growth, not manufacture it.
