THE FIRST FLAGSHIP GLOBAL LEARNING CENTER

ETHS is where YTC will prove what a fully connected club can become.

YTC@ETHS is being developed as YTC’s first Flagship Global Learning Center: a working demonstration of how one strong school club can connect student learning, local community service, universities, global peers and future digital tools.

The flagship is not a national headquarters, and this remains the local ETHS chapter site. It is the first place where the wider YTC model will be assembled, tested and made visible—so that future clubs can learn from something real.

ETHS students will help shape the systems that follow: peer teaching, verified service and teaching hours, the student portal, global learning experiences and the planned bYTCoin recognition pilot.

THE FUNDING BRIDGE

The long-term plan has not changed. The present reality has.

YTC has always intended each club to become locally self-sufficient, while major donors and larger grants support the shared organization. With only two main clubs operating in the United States, that larger funding base has not yet arrived at the scale the work requires.

THE YTC@ETHS COMMUNITY TEAM

You do not have to teach robotics to strengthen the club.

YTC@ETHS is student-led, but students should not have to carry every adult responsibility around the program. We are building a small community advisory team of parents, alumni and Evanston professionals who can take ownership of a defined need and help the club grow.

Marketing + storytelling

Help turn real student work into clear stories, photographs, video, news and community awareness.

Design + digital

Help improve the website, visual materials, presentations and the tools students and supporters use.

Fundraising + grants

Help identify opportunities, prepare proposals, build donor relationships and strengthen local support.

Partnerships + opportunities

Connect ETHS students with universities, companies, nonprofits, speakers, mentors and meaningful experiences.

Equipment + operations

Help source, organize and move useful technology, supplies and the practical resources that keep projects working.

Mentors + speakers

Share a skill, career path, idea or experience that can widen what an ETHS student imagines doing next.

This is an advisory working team, not a second governing board and not an open-ended volunteer commitment. The invitation is simple: bring a useful skill, choose a defined need and help carry it.

bYTCoin · Planned Pilot

FUND THE MODEL BEFORE LAUNCH

Let ETHS students help build a system future clubs can use.

The planned winter pilot will begin at ETHS only after it is funded. Students will help shape how learning, teaching, mentoring and participation are recognized, then test a model that can be adapted by future YTC clubs worldwide.

Funding will support the student ledger, portal tools, pilot experiences, program administration and evaluation needed to launch responsibly. Local businesses, families and supporters will also be able to contribute experiences and rewards—not only cash.

Movie passes
Gift certificates
Local discounts
Tickets + outings
Food + club events
Donated experiences
Important: bYTCoin will not be cryptocurrency, cash or a way to purchase advancement. Teaching hours and leadership progress must still be verified. The pilot is designed to recognize growth, not manufacture it.

KEEP IT LOCAL + MANAGEABLE

The community contributes. The club decides what fits.

YTC school-club leadership would work with program facilitators to manage the local support and recognition program. That keeps decisions close to the students and keeps the system from becoming another administrative burden.

Club leadership

Helps identify useful experiences, meaningful milestones and what students actually value.

Program facilitators

Help manage the practical side: available contributions, redemption opportunities, timing and fit with the program.
