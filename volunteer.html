An invitation from Mark Lennon

Learn how a nonprofit works by helping run one.

I'm putting together a small group of ETHS students to help with the work that keeps YTC growing and connected to its communities.

I was executive director of a century-old settlement house, where I spent ten years bringing recent college graduates and graduate students into nonprofit work. I want to bring that experience to students here at ETHS.

You would work with me during my day at the school on YTC projects: planning an event, getting people to show up, creating the marketing, organizing volunteers, or keeping the budget and records in order.

You'll have a responsibility of your own, guidance as you learn, and something you can point to and say: I helped make that happen.

Mark LennonExecutive Vice President, Youth Technology Corps

A project you could help lead

A computer collection day needs a location, a clear invitation, volunteers, a collection plan, records, and thank-yous. You could help take that project from an idea to an event that serves the community.
