New this year · Debuting at ETHS

Welcome to the YTC Student Portal.

A new feature for YTC clubs, launching first here at Evanston Township High School — built for our program, and built to connect us to the wider world.

▶ The welcome video is on its way. Everything it covers is written out below — take two minutes and read on.

An introduction from Mark Lennon, Executive Vice President, Youth Technology Corps.

If you’re a student — or just curious about the portal — read on. If not, close this page and enjoy the site. We hope it puts a face on work we can all be proud of.

Welcome back

A new year at ETHS, and a new experience in the Clubroom.

We built a student portal that does everything our old club site did — and a good deal more. It is the daily home for every member of YTC@ETHS.

The first time you go in, we’ll ask you a few questions to set up your profile. After that, when you sign in you’ll land on a welcome screen that already knows you.

It knows where you are

Your profile recognizes you, marks your attendance, and follows your progress through the curriculum.

It counts your hours

Volunteer hours are logged as you go — no clipboard, no trying to remember in June.

It opens doors

Learning opportunities we think will help you grow, as a student and as a person figuring out what you’re into.

No pressure

All of it is optional.

You’re here to find your own path, learn at your own pace, bounce between STEM subjects, and just explore. Nothing here is a requirement or a grade.

We are a club first — and a global learning center for those who choose to use it that way.

The reward system

Say hello to BYTCOIN.

BYTE-COIN— and if you look closely, YTC is right there in the middle of it.

Right now YTC@ETHS runs on a very small budget, so BYTCOIN starts small too — worth just a few cents. But it will grow, and we will grow it together. The goal is for BYTCOIN to carry real value to you, with real-world rewards.

You earn it for the things that matter: showing up, watching the week’s video, answering the question of the week, teaching someone else.

An idea we’re floating

An overnight laser tag trip, end of the school year.

Here’s the proposal: it would be open to everyone. Anyone with enough BYTCOIN could go free, if they choose to spend it — and anyone else could buy in. We’ll set pricing once we have a date.

This is an experiment for us. Systems like it run in youth programs all over the world, in one form or another. Pay attention, and take advantage of what’s on offer.

Every week

A video, and a question.

Video of the Week

Watch at home, on your phone or your computer. It might be a YTC story, a TED Talk, a news clip, or an Instagram reel. Answer a few questions about it and you earn BYTCOIN for the time you put in.

Question of the Week

We want to know what you think — your interests, your hobbies, your favorite Evanston place to eat. It tells the club staff how to make the Clubroom better and put opportunities in front of you that actually mean something.

The coursework

Every lesson, always available.

All of the course work lives in the portal. Follow along on a computer or a phone during class when we’re running virtual learning — or go back and review something, or jump ahead if you’re ready. It’s all there.

For mentors

Once you qualify, the portal is where you sign in, set up your class, and get credit for the work — whether you’re teaching, mentoring, or tracking a class locally or on the other side of the world.

We hope this opens up the world to you.

Our first Video of the Week is a TED Talk we picked as a bit of inspiration to start the year. And if you see something here that could be better — just ask.
