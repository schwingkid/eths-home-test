Welcome back

A new year at ETHS, and a new experience in the Clubroom.

We built a student portal that does everything our old club site did — and a good deal more. It is the daily home for every member of YTC@ETHS.

The first time you go in, we’ll ask you a few questions to set up your profile. After that, when you sign in you’ll land on a welcome screen that already knows you.

It knows where you are

Your profile recognizes you, marks your attendance, and follows your progress through the curriculum.

It counts your hours

Volunteer hours are logged as you go — no clipboard, no trying to remember in June.

It opens doors

Learning opportunities we think will help you grow, as a student and as a person figuring out what you’re into.

No pressure

All of it is optional.

You’re here to find your own path, learn at your own pace, bounce between STEM subjects, and just explore. Nothing here is a requirement or a grade.

We are a club first — and a global learning center for those who choose to use it that way.

The reward system

Meet your IMPACT Account.

YTC IMPACTInitiative · Mastery · Problem-solving · Agency · Contribution · Teaching

Your IMPACT Account is a growing record of what you learn, teach, lead and contribute. Inside the portal, completed tasks can earn IMPACT Credits — for checking in, completing the week’s activity, mastering a skill, teaching someone else, serving on a project or taking responsibility.

Credits are recognition, not money. They have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold or transferred.

What the account can open

Recognition that follows real work.

As your account grows, it can help unlock badges, certificates, leadership roles, special learning experiences and donor-funded opportunities. Credits remain in the account as a record — they are not spent like cash.

Students will help design the rules so the system reflects what matters in this club.

Every week

A video, and a question.

Video of the Week

Watch at home, on your phone or your computer. It might be a YTC story, a TED Talk, a news clip, or an Instagram reel. Complete the activity and IMPACT Credits are added to your account for the work you put in.

Question of the Week

We want to know what you think — your interests, your hobbies, your favorite Evanston place to eat. It tells the club staff how to make the Clubroom better and put opportunities in front of you that actually mean something.

The coursework

Every lesson, always available.

All of the course work lives in the portal. Follow along on a computer or a phone during class when we’re running virtual learning — or go back and review something, or jump ahead if you’re ready. It’s all there.

For mentors

Once you qualify, the portal is where you sign in, set up your class, and get credit for the work — whether you’re teaching, mentoring, or tracking a class locally or on the other side of the world.

We hope this opens up the world to you.
